# Seed 713 maintenance interchange

Run `Launch-Interchange-Study.cmd` on Windows, or run `node serve.cjs 8794` and open `/studies/maintenance-interchange/index.html`. Choose either entrance. Click the view to use WASD and arrows. Shift holds survey pace. Each entrance starts just outside its wall opening. The ramp-foot buttons skip the long approach. Cross-biome ownership and the other transport classes still belong to the 1.8.1 Observatory.

Both spirals use the same two-turn design mirrored across the freight corridor. The two grade-level approaches feed straight into the lower curves. At the summit, the last level chord points directly along the bridge on both sides. The 3.5 km crossing has no elevated feeder viaduct. A 12 m deck leaves 18 m over the freight.

The study opens at the Meadow summit. Use the summit buttons to compare both approaches or the entrance buttons to walk from the wall. Each 36-second walking film crosses one summit at 1.5 m/s and holds at the end.

Run `node tests/maintenance-interchange.mjs` for the route, tangent and mesh reuse checks. Run `node tools/film-maintenance-interchange.cjs films/interchange` to record the two summit walks and the aerial survey.
