# Fly past Bay +1

On ALPHA, double-click **I:\Sphere\Launch-Sphere-Continuous-Borders.cmd**. It opens Bay +1 on an available local port. Allow about a minute for a first visit. When the opening bar clears, click the flight view.

1. **Hold W for six seconds.** You start at +212 km, facing along the wall at 2 km/s. The old end passes beneath you after two seconds.
2. **Release W, hold Q until descent stops, then tap F.** Walk on the plain roof at 1,746.7 m. Drag to turn around and look back at Double Gallery.
3. **Mark your landing** in **Explore → Atlas places → Mark this place**. Hold **Space** and tap **E** to lift off, climb with **E**, then fly with **W**. **Plain wall beyond Bay +1** restores the tested landing at +305.34 km.

**At the corner.** Run `I:\Sphere\Launch-Sphere-Continuous-Borders.cmd junction` to start 9 km before the turn. The mark **Junction · east arm looking back** opens the view from the next border.

**Into Super Jungle.** Run `I:\Sphere\Launch-Sphere-Continuous-Borders.cmd doors` for the Bay 0 service door. Use 128× through the 48 km gallery and return to 1× near the far door. The clearing is 412 m outside. The buttressed tree is another 100 m outward and 60 m left. **Bay 0 Jungle buttress** returns you there.

**Into Violet.** Choose **Violet fork approach at 11.355 km** under Atlas places. Follow the pale fork with **W** at 1.2 km/s. At 1.4 km remaining, slow down, aim along the incoming arm, descend with **Q** and land with **F**. **Violet mineral fork** returns you on foot.

Both habitats are installed. On a fresh seed-713 register, install **Infinite Meadow**, **Border Biome**, **Super Jungle**, and **Violet Labyrinth** under **Explore → Walkable biome packs**. The optional **Install reference grove** button adds a second Jungle.

For the Jungle-side pavement, use **Bay 0 Jungle buttress**, lift off with **Space + E**, and look back toward Bay 0 from about 835 m. The wide Monumental door and the narrow Service door both open onto paved approaches. Walk back to the marked buttress to inspect the bark, young broadleaves and low leaves in the trunk fork.

On a plain wall the readout names its Atlas edge, distance from that edge’s midpoint, the nearest bay and the shortest distance **by wall** to its centre. At the photographed Violet position that bay is **Bay −1**, roughly **409.25 million km** along connected roofs. For a quick comparison, standing on the approach at **216.1 km** beyond Bay 0 puts you **16.1 km** from Bay +1. At a steady manual 1,000 km/s the wall trip from Violet to Bay -1 takes about 4 days 18 hours. The centre reticle in Main measures the distance along your sightline.

**Floor walk.** Use the `doors` launcher route. Walk the Service deck and look down by the amber guide to see traction bars, plate fasteners and the grated threshold. The open freight floor has larger concrete pours. Fly along the through-lane into the connecting tunnel to follow its joints and wheel paths across the sill.

**Under the cranes.** In the Transfer Yard at Bay -1, follow the freight line beneath the opening in the roof. Measured from the freight floor to the top lamp, the three cranes stand 2,492 m, 3,052 m, and 2,582 m tall in order of increasing distance along the hall. The middle crane stands 1,307 m above the roof deck.
