## IB-050 — Native instanced-environment pass

## IB-060 — Deterministic streamed grass patches

**Outcome:** resident grass follows the observer while the meadow remains fixed.

- Generate blade roots from fixed geographic patch and subcell addresses.
  Randomise position, yaw, height, width, species/color class and wind phase
  through independent deterministic streams.
- Reject or modify roots using the semantic field: water, exposed rock, slope,
  path/structure clearances, soil wetness, damage and canopy.
- Build compact instance buffers in workers. Reuse allocations where practical,
  but never reuse randomness from the observer's current location.
- Conform roots and normals to authoritative terrain. Use world-stable wind
  coordinates that retain precision after render-origin rebasing.
- Treat footprints and temporary flattening as transient fields unless a later
  recorded-world contract explicitly persists them.

**Required evidence:** unload/revisit, cold reload, reversed traversal, rebase
and different worker order produce identical blade/cluster hashes; water and
path exclusions agree with visible ground; leaving a region releases its
resources without a visible hole in the support surface.

nearby patches occupy CPU and GPU memory.

**Completion record · 19 September 2026:** The exit gate passed. The
