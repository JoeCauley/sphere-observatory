# Projects alongside The Sphere Observatory

Public-source comparison researched 12 September 2026. Others are building substantial parts of this idea. This search did not establish an exact match for the Observatory's combination; it cannot prove that none exists.

| Project | Shared ground | Different emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| [Another Sky](https://dysonswarm.com/another-sky/) | A browser-based artificial habitat with walking, flight, atlas travel, lighting, collision and photo capture | A 24 km-wide, 56 km-long O'Neill cylinder. Its author describes an imagined habitat, not an orbital-physics simulation. The closest small-project habitat analogue found. |
| [SpaceEngine](https://spaceengine.org/) | Realistically scaled exploration, seamless travel from planetary surfaces into space, procedural worlds and saved locations | A universe of natural celestial worlds. It reaches vastly greater overall distances than Sphere; Sphere concentrates on the interior and inhabitants' possible experience of one constructed world. |
| [Universe Sandbox](https://universesandbox.com/faq/) | An interactive environment for investigating physical consequences | Gravity, temperature, climate and celestial collisions are central. A useful physics benchmark, while Sphere currently concentrates on exploration and visualization inside an authored megastructure. |
| [Outerra](https://www.outerra.com/) | Planetary rendering, procedural terrain, local exploration and a canvas for user creations | A planetary engine and sandbox rather than a stellar-scale inhabited shell. Relevant to keeping distant geography and local experience coherent. |
| [Orion's Arm](https://www.orionsarm.com/) | Hard-science worldbuilding, megastructures, cultures and storytelling across a shared setting | Primarily a collaborative fictional universe expressed through reference material, writing and art. Sphere aims to make its setting spatially explorable and measurable in a running app. |
| [Dyson Sphere Program](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1366540/Dyson_Sphere_Program/) | Constructed stellar megastructures and a space sandbox | Factory automation and industrial expansion, rather than exploring life inside an inhabited shell. |
| [Orbiter](https://github.com/orbitersim/orbiter) | Open-source solar-system exploration with Newtonian spaceflight | A spacecraft and dynamics simulator. Relevant to future physical travel models, while Sphere's present free camera is not a spacecraft. |

The [RingWorld Minecraft mod](https://github.com/Delaser/RingWorld) is another useful rendering reference: visible curvature, wraparound travel and terrain represented across the ring. Its [current-state documentation](https://github.com/Delaser/RingWorld/blob/main/docs/CURRENT_STATE.md) identifies vanilla Minecraft gravity, so it is not evidence of equivalent physical simulation.

## Sphere's opportunity

The closest broad exploration benchmark is SpaceEngine; the closest habitat experience found is Another Sky; the closest worldbuilding philosophy is Orion's Arm. Sphere's opportunity is to bring those threads together around one coherent stellar-scale world: physically grounded geometry and light, travel down to human-scale places, and reproducible exploration that can support stories.

This is an interpretation of the projects' public descriptions, not an independent technical audit or claim that Sphere already exceeds them. Several have much deeper implementations in their own areas. Sphere still assumes shell support, gravity, heat disposal and atmosphere retention, prescribes Shade motion, and approximates weather and indirect light. Its distinctive ambition does not require presenting those unsolved systems as complete.
